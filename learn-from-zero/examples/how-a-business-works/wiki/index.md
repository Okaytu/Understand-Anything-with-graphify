# How a business works — index

A beginner's map, built from zero around one example: **Corner Coffee**, a small café.
Read top to bottom; each section builds on the one before.

## Foundations
- [[Business Model]] — how the business creates, delivers and captures value
- [[Customers]] — who pays, and why
- [[Value Proposition]] — the promise that makes customers choose you

## Money
- [[Revenue]] — money coming in
- [[Pricing]] — deciding what to charge
- [[Costs]] — money going out, fixed and variable
- [[Gross Margin]] — what is left from each sale after its direct costs
- [[Break-even Point]] — how much you must sell to stop losing money
- [[Cash Flow]] — when money actually arrives and leaves
- [[Taxes]] — the government's share, and when it is due

## Operations
- [[Suppliers]] — who provides what you sell
- [[Operations]] — the daily work of making and delivering
- [[Hiring]] — adding people, and what they must earn back

## Growth
- [[Marketing]] — getting customers to know and try you
- [[Unit Economics]] — whether one customer is worth what it costs to win them
- [[Competitors]] — the other choices your customers have
