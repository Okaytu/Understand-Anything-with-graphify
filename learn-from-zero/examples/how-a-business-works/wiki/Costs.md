# Costs

Costs are the money going out. Split them into two kinds:
- **Variable costs** grow with every sale: beans, milk, cups. These set the [[Gross Margin]].
- **Fixed costs** are paid whatever you sell: rent, salaries, insurance, equipment loans.

**Corner Coffee:** variable cost about $1.20 per drink. Fixed costs about $16,000 a month
(rent $5,000, staff $9,500, other $1,500). Staff is the cost that grows with [[Hiring]].

Fixed costs decide the [[Break-even Point]]. [[Suppliers]] and [[Operations]] decide most
variable costs.
