# Operations

Operations is the day-to-day work that turns inputs from [[Suppliers]] into what
[[Customers]] receive, and keeps the [[Value Proposition]] true.

**Corner Coffee:** two baristas at peak hours, one off-peak; a target of under three minutes
per order; daily stock checks so nothing runs out.

Good operations lower [[Costs]] (less waste, right staffing) and protect [[Revenue]] (no lost
sales to long queues).
