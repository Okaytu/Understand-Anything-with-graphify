# Gross Margin

Gross margin is what is left from each sale after its variable [[Costs]]:
gross margin = price − variable cost per unit, often shown as a percentage of price.

**Corner Coffee:** $4.50 − $1.20 = $3.30 per drink, a 73% gross margin.

That $3.30 is what pays the fixed costs. Once those are covered, the rest is profit.
This is the key number behind the [[Break-even Point]] and [[Unit Economics]]. Better
[[Pricing]] or cheaper [[Suppliers]] raise it.
