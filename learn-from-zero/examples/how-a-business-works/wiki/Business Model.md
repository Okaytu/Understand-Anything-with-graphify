# Business Model

A business model is the short story of how a business makes money: who its [[Customers]] are,
what [[Value Proposition]] it offers them, how it delivers that through [[Operations]], and how
the [[Revenue]] it earns ends up bigger than its [[Costs]].

**Corner Coffee:** sells good coffee and a quiet place to work to people in the neighbourhood.
It buys beans and milk from [[Suppliers]], turns them into drinks, and charges more per cup
than each cup costs to make. That difference is its [[Gross Margin]].

Questions to ask about any business:
- Who pays, and for what?
- What does it cost to deliver one unit?
- What has to be true for this to be profitable? See [[Break-even Point]].
