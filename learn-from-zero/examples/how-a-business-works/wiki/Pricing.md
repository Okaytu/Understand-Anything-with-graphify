# Pricing

Pricing is choosing what to charge. Three common anchors:
1. **Cost-plus:** cost per unit plus a target [[Gross Margin]].
2. **Competitor-based:** near what alternatives charge.
3. **Value-based:** what the [[Value Proposition]] is worth to the [[Customers]].

**Corner Coffee:** a latte costs about $1.20 to make. Chains charge $5.00. The café charges
$4.50: well above cost, a little below the chain, justified by speed and quality.

A higher price raises [[Revenue]] per sale but can lose customers. A lower price needs more
sales to pass the [[Break-even Point]].
