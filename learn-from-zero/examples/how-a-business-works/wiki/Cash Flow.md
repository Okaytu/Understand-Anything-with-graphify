# Cash Flow

Cash flow is the timing of money in and out. A profitable business can still run out of cash
if it pays [[Suppliers]] and rent before customers pay it.

**Corner Coffee** is lucky: [[Customers]] pay at the till, while suppliers give 30 days to pay.
Cash comes in before it goes out. A catering contract paid 60 days after delivery would flip that.

Watch it together with [[Revenue]] and [[Costs]]: profit says *whether* you earn money, cash
flow says *whether you can pay the bills this week*.
