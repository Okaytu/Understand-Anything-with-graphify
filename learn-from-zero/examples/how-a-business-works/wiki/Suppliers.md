# Suppliers

Suppliers sell you the inputs you turn into your product. Their prices set most variable
[[Costs]], and their payment terms shape [[Cash Flow]].

**Corner Coffee** buys beans from a local roaster ($0.45 per drink), milk from a dairy
($0.35), cups and lids ($0.25), and pays $0.15 per drink in other supplies.

A 10% cheaper bean contract adds about $0.05 to the [[Gross Margin]] of every drink.
Reliability matters too: a missed delivery stops [[Operations]].
