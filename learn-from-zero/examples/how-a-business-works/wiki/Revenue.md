# Revenue

Revenue is all the money customers pay you, before any [[Costs]].
Revenue = number of sales × average price.

**Corner Coffee:** 220 drinks a day × $4.50 average = about $990 a day, $29,700 a month.

Revenue is not profit. Subtract [[Costs]] to see what is left, and look at [[Cash Flow]]
to see *when* the money really arrives. You grow revenue by winning more [[Customers]],
raising [[Pricing]], or selling more to each customer.
