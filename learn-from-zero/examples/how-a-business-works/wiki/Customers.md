# Customers

Customers are the people or organisations who pay. Knowing exactly who they are shapes
[[Pricing]], [[Marketing]] and the [[Value Proposition]].

**Corner Coffee** has two groups:
- *Morning commuters*: want speed, buy one drink, come daily.
- *Remote workers*: stay for hours, buy two or three items, care about Wi-Fi and seats.

Different customers bring different [[Revenue]] and cost different amounts to serve, which is
why [[Unit Economics]] looks at them one at a time.
