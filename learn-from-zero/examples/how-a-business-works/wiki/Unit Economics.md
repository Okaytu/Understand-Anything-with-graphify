# Unit Economics

Unit economics asks whether one unit of business, often one customer, is profitable on its own.
Two numbers:
- **CAC (customer acquisition cost):** [[Marketing]] spend ÷ new customers won.
- **LTV (lifetime value):** [[Gross Margin]] per visit × visits per year × years they stay.

**Corner Coffee:** CAC = $7,200 a year ÷ 120 regulars = $60.
LTV = $3.30 × 150 visits × 2 years ≈ $990. LTV is about 16× CAC, so winning customers pays off.

If LTV were below CAC, growth through [[Marketing]] would *lose* money: see [[Business Model]].
