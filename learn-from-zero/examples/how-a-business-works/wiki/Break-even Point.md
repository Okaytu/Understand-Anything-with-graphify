# Break-even Point

The break-even point is how much you must sell to cover all your [[Costs]], so you are
neither losing nor making money.
break-even units = fixed costs ÷ [[Gross Margin]] per unit.

**Corner Coffee:** $16,000 ÷ $3.30 ≈ 4,850 drinks a month, about 162 a day. It sells 220,
so it makes a profit of roughly 58 × $3.30 ≈ $190 a day.

Levers: raise [[Pricing]], cut variable costs with [[Suppliers]], cut fixed costs, or sell more
through [[Marketing]].
