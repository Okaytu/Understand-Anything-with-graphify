# Value Proposition

The value proposition is the reason a customer picks you over the alternatives: your
[[Competitors]], or doing nothing. It must matter to your [[Customers]] and be something your [[Operations]]
can actually deliver.

**Corner Coffee:** *great coffee in under three minutes, and a calm place to work.*
It lets the café charge a little more than a chain ([[Pricing]]) and gives [[Marketing]]
something specific to say.
