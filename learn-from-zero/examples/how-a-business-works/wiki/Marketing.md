# Marketing

Marketing is how new [[Customers]] hear about you and decide to try you, and how existing
ones come back. Each new customer has a cost, which [[Unit Economics]] compares with what
that customer is worth.

**Corner Coffee:** a loyalty card (10th drink free), a sign for commuters, and a $600-a-month
local social-media budget that brings about 120 new regulars a year.

Marketing should repeat the [[Value Proposition]] and drive enough sales to stay past the
[[Break-even Point]].
